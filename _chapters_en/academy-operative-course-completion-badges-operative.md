## 🏅 Earn your Operative badge

Every Agent Academy learning path — **Recruit → Operative → Commander** — includes an official digital badge issued through the [Global AI Community](https://globalai.community/).

This badge is:

- Verifiable
- Shareable on LinkedIn, resumes, and portfolios
- Connected to real technical work, not just participation

### 🧭 Submission process

Follow **all steps below in the exact order**.

### 1. ⭐ Star the Agent Academy GitHub Repo

👉 **[Agent Academy GitHub Repo](https://github.com/microsoft/agent-academy)**

**Why this matters:**

- It helps others discover the Academy more easily.
- It demonstrates active community participation.
- It directly supports continued investment in the project.

Starring the repository is quick, free, and required for badge eligibility.

### 2. 🧾 Complete the badge validation form

👉 **[Badge Validation Form](https://aka.ms/agent-academy-operative/form)**

This form does the following.

- Confirms that you completed the curriculum through questions about the Operative path.
- Collects feedback so Agent Academy can keep improving.
- Confirms the correct email address for sending the badge.
- Lets you consent to receiving the badge through the Global AI Community.

Yes, it is a separate form. Yes, it is required.  
This process keeps the system scalable as more participants join.

### 3. 🔐 Create and sign in to a Global AI Community account

👉 **[Global AI Community Account Log In](https://globalai.community/auth/login)**

Badges are issued **through the Global AI Community platform**.

If you do not have an account:

- Create a free account using the **same email address** you entered in the badge request form. Otherwise, you will not receive the badge.

No account = no badge, even if your submission is valid.

<div class="info-box note" markdown="1">
**Important** — Make sure the email addresses match and that you entered the email correctly. Otherwise, the badge will not be delivered.
</div>
