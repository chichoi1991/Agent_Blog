## 🏅 Claim your Recruit badge

Every Agent Academy path (**Recruit → Operative → Commander**) has an official digital badge issued through the [Global AI Community](https://globalai.community/).

This badge is:

- Verifiable
- Shareable (LinkedIn, résumé, portfolio, and more)
- Based on real technical work, not just attendance

To preserve the meaning of the badge, Agent Academy follows a **strict validation process**.

### 🧭 Submission process

Complete **all** steps below **in order**.

### 1. ⭐ Star the Agent Academy GitHub repository

👉 [Agent Academy GitHub Repo](https://github.com/microsoft/agent-academy)

**Why does this matter?**

- It helps more people discover the Academy.
- It signals active community participation.
- It directly supports continued investment in the project.

Starring is quick, free, and part of the badge eligibility requirements.

### 2. 🧾 Complete the badge validation form

👉 [Badge Validation Form](https://aka.ms/agent-academy-recruit/form)

This form:

- Confirms actual curriculum completion with questions about the Recruit path
- Collects feedback to improve Agent Academy
- Collects the correct email address for receiving your badge
- Confirms your consent to receive the badge through the Global AI Community

### 3. 🔐 Create and sign in to your Global AI Community account

👉 [Global AI Community Account Log In](https://globalai.community/auth/login)

Badges are issued through the **Global AI Community platform**.

If you do not have an account, make sure that you:

- Create a free account with the **same email address** you entered in the badge request form. If the email addresses do not match, the badge cannot be delivered.

If you do not have an account, your badge will not be issued even if your submission is valid.

<div class="info-box note" markdown="1">
**Important**

**Be sure to verify that the email address matches and that you entered it correctly. Otherwise, your badge will not be delivered.**
</div>
