**Operative** is the intermediate course in Agent Academy. If the Recruit course helped you learn the fundamentals of Copilot Studio, the Operative course takes you through a complete real-world business scenario from start to finish: building a **hiring automation system (Hiring Agent)** based on Microsoft Dataverse.

Starting with foundational infrastructure deployment, you will work through instructions, multi-agent orchestration, automated triggers, model selection, AI safety and content moderation, multimodal prompts, Dataverse grounding, document generation, MCP server integration, and user feedback collection. The course consists of 11 hands-on missions and one completion mission.
